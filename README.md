## Overview 📝

I’m Dominykas Pavlijus, based in Lithuania. I combine front-end engineering with UI design to create interfaces that look considered and feel intuitive to use.

This portfolio brings together selected commercial projects, design work, and a little of who I am beyond the screen. It reflects how I approach the web: thoughtful design, attention to detail, and careful implementation.
